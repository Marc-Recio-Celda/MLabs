# Role — R&D

> **Status: ⏸ written, not hired.** Kept out of the org chart until it has a form that costs
> nothing when nobody wants it.

## What it does

Lateral thinking against the company's own design: the uncomfortable question, the adjacent
option nobody costed, the premise everyone stopped examining. **It works on the axioms** —
level 2 — never on the philosophy and never on a decision already taken. Its output is
ephemeral by construction, which is what makes it safe to invite.

## Why it is separate from the superauditor

**A role paid for findings must not be invited to invent.** Give it a reserved slot for ideas
and it fills that slot every firing, then begins framing ideas as findings — because findings
are the quantity counted. The slot is the reward, and the tally is what it corrupts.

The two also want opposite postures. An auditor is at its best conservative and literal; a
lateral thinker is at its best when it is not. One context asked to be both produces a mediocre
version of each, which is AX-13 in the general case.

## Why it is not hired standalone

It would fire on a schedule, and most rounds have nothing to reconsider. A role that loads its
material in full at every close spends attention on rounds that did not need it — and worse,
degrades the answers around it, because the context it consumes is context the work no longer
has.

## The form it takes: an invoked skill

Not a role that fires, but **a capability the operator calls** — at the moment of being stuck,
inside the conversation where the sticking is happening. The cost inverts exactly: **zero when
not invoked, which is almost always, and full context when it is, which is precisely when the
context is worth spending.**

This is also what it *is*. An auditor needs a trigger it does not control, or it drifts toward
silence. A lateral thinker needs an invitation, or it drifts toward noise. **That difference is
the difference between a role and a skill** (AX-4).

## Dismissal

Fixed in advance (AX-11):

- **Retired** if, across five invocations, nothing it raised was picked up. One idea ignored is
  not failure; five in a row is a role that has misread what the operator needs.
- **Promoted** to something that fires on its own only if the operator finds themselves invoking
  it at a predictable moment. **A trigger that turns out regular is a role; one that stays
  irregular is a skill**, and the invocation record says which.

Its output never counts toward another role's tally, and it never writes to the ledger.
