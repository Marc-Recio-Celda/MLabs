# Role — R&D

> **Status: ⏸ written and deactivated.** Not a vacancy waiting for the hiring rule (`AGENTS.md`
> §6) — a role deliberately kept out of the org chart until it has a form that costs nothing
> when nobody wants it. It is written down because **what was considered and set aside has no
> other record** (AX-24), and because the next person to think "we need a creative role" should
> find the reasoning rather than repeat the experiment.

## What it would do

Lateral thinking against the company's own design: the uncomfortable question, the adjacent
option nobody costed, the premise everyone stopped examining. **It works on the axioms** — level
2 — proposing what could be improved, never on the philosophy and never on a decision already
taken. And it is **never annoyed at being ignored**: its output is ephemeral by construction,
which is what makes it safe to invite.

## Why it is not merged into the superauditor

It was, for one round, and the merge was reversed before either had run. The argument that
killed it:

> **You do not pay a role for findings and also invite it to invent.** Given a reserved slot for
> ideas, a role measured by what it catches will fill that slot every time, and then begin
> dressing ideas as findings — because that is the quantity being counted. This is not a risk of
> the merge; it is the merge's incentive working exactly as designed.

And there is a second reason, softer but real: **the two stances want different postures.** An
auditor is at its best when it is conservative and literal. A lateral thinker is at its best
when it is not. Asking one context to be both produces a mediocre version of each, which is what
AX-13 says in the general case.

## Why it is not hired standalone either

Because it would fire on a schedule, and most rounds do not need it. A role that runs every
close, over material it must load in full, spends attention on rounds that had nothing to
reconsider — and PH-6 is not a preference. Worse, it would degrade the answers around it: the
context it consumes is context the actual work no longer has.

## The form it is likely to take: an invoked skill

Not a role that fires, but **a capability the operator calls** — at the moment of being stuck on
a problem, inside the conversation where the sticking is happening. That inverts the cost
exactly: **zero when not invoked, which is almost always, and full context when it is, which is
precisely when the context is worth spending.**

It is also the honest reading of what this thing is. An auditor must fire without being asked,
or it drifts toward silence. A lateral thinker asked to speak on a schedule drifts toward noise.
**One needs a trigger it does not control; the other needs an invitation** — and that difference
is the difference between a role and a skill (AX-4).

## Dismissal criterion — fixed now, before it ever runs

Recorded in advance so it cannot be re-read favourably later (AX-11). Whatever form it takes:

- **Retired** if, across five invocations, nothing it raised was picked up. An idea nobody takes
  is not a failure; five in a row is a role that has misread what the operator needs.
- **Promoted** — from invoked skill to something that fires on its own — only if the operator
  finds themselves invoking it at a predictable moment. **A trigger that turns out to be
  regular is a role; one that stays irregular is a skill**, and the invocation record is what
  says which.

Its output is never counted toward any other role's tally, and it never writes to the ledger.
